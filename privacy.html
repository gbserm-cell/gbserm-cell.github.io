Privacy Policy

rclone-gdrive is a personal OAuth client used only by its owner for backing up and syncing the owner's own Google Drive files with rclone.

  • The app does not collect, store, sell or share any data of third parties.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Data from Google Drive is accessed only to perform the owner's own backup and sync tasks and is not sent to any other service.
